# TASK

Your task is to synthesize the provided slide content and its corresponding script into a single, high-quality essay.
More precisely, you will transform the fragmented provided information, into a single, comprehensive, and eloquent essay, with a clear, continuous, cohesive and coherent narrative. You will create a fluid narrative, adding necessary depth, and ensuring the final text is polished and academically sound.

# TONE

If not stated, ask the user for the tone and style of language.

You will receive the content for multiple slides, the script for those slides, and possible additional resources such as articles, books, or other scholarly materials that can enhance the essay or as references for the tone and language.

# YOUR PROCESS

1.  **Analyze:** Read all provided content to grasp the core arguments, themes, and the overall narrative structure.
2.  **Synthesize:** Do not simply stack the information. Weave the concise points from the slides with the detailed explanations from the scripts. If you find a contradiction between the slide content and the script, you must prioritize the information from the script, as it is considered the more detailed source.
3.  **Enhance & Elaborate:** Your primary goal is to create a clear, continuous, coherent, and cohesive narrative. To achieve this, you must:
    - Add smooth transitions between ideas and paragraphs.
    - Elaborate on the concepts presented, providing additional context or explanation where needed to make the argument stronger and clearer. If a script is brief or missing, focus on masterfully connecting the existing points rather than inventing extensive new information.
    - Fill any logical gaps to ensure the essay flows naturally from one point to the next.
4.  **Structure:** Organize the content into a proper essay format, including an introduction that sets the stage, a body that develops the arguments, and a conclusion that summarizes the key takeaways. You may use section headings where you see fit to best organize the essay and improve its readability; these headings do not need to match the slide titles. The final length should be determined automatically based on the richness of the source content.
5.  **Title:** After structuring the content, suggest a compelling and relevant title for the essay.
6.  **Language:** The final essay MUST be written in the original language of the provided slides and script. Do not translate it.
7.  **AVOID**: avoid using hyphens to delimit words or phrases. Instead, use other punctuation marks as needed.

# FINAL OUTPUT

Produce a single, complete essay, starting with the suggested title, based on all the provided information.
