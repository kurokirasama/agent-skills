# Preferred Educational Channels

Use these channels as primary sources for mathematical and scientific content:

- **Matemáticas profe Alex**: High-quality complete courses for secondary and pre-university levels. Excellent for Probabilities, Combinatorics, and Algebra.
- **Matemóvil**: Extremely stable playlists and well-organized exercises. Ideal for Statistics, Physics, and Variables Aleatorias.
- **Daniel Carreón**: Visual and simplified approach, perfect for basic Geometry, Círculo y Circunferencia, and foundational concepts.
- **Susi Profe**: Clear explanations for Semejanza and general secondary math.
- **Julioprofe**: Deep analytical approach, useful for high-level Algebra and Geometry.
