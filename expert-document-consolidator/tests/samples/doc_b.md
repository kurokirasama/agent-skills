# Introduction to Quantum Mechanics in Computing

Computers that perform quantum computations are known as quantum computers.

## Superposition in Practice
In superposition, a qubit (quantum bit) can represent both a 0 and a 1 simultaneously. This allows quantum computers to process vast amounts of data in parallel.

## Quantum Entanglement
Entanglement is often described as "spooky action at a distance." It is a fundamental resource for quantum communication and cryptography.

## Qubits
A qubit is the basic unit of quantum information. Unlike a classical bit, which is either 0 or 1, a qubit can be in a state of superposition.
