**General Instructions:**

- **Tone:** The tone should be formal, educational, and respectful.
- **Audience:** The target audience is a general audience with an interest in religious and biblical studies.

**Visual Style:**

- **Layout:**
    - **Title Slide:** The title slide should have a large, centered title and a subtitle.
    - **Content Slides:** Content slides should have a clear title at the top. Use a two-column layout when appropriate to present information in a more organized way. Use content boxes with a semi-transparent black background and a light-colored border to group related information.
    - **Lists:** Use bullet points for lists. 
- **Visual Elements:**
    - **Images:** Include relevant images to illustrate the concepts presented. The images should be high-quality and have a professional look.
    - **Diagrams:** Use diagrams to explain complex concepts. The diagrams should be simple, clear, and easy to understand.

**Presentation Structure:**

1.  **Title Slide:** The first slide should be the title of the presentation.
2.  **Introduction:** An introductory slide that presents the topic.
3.  **Content Slides:** A series of slides that present the main content, with titles, bullet points, images, and diagrams.
4.  **Conclusion:** A concluding slide that summarizes the main points.
5.  **Q&A/Discussion:** A final slide to open the floor for questions and discussion. Include 2-4 questions or discussion topics related to the topic of the presentation.
